about=Generates soil sampling plans from a field boundary polygon (a layer loaded in QGIS or a shapefile). Three methods are available: square cells, regular point grid and hexagons, driven by the sampling density in hectares per sample. Outputs sample points (numbered in a serpentine path) and sampling cells, reprojected to UTM when the input is geographic. Interface in Portuguese (pt-BR).
